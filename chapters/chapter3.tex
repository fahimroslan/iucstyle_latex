\section{Mathematical Formulation}

The Laplace transform of the time-weighted cosine function is
\begin{equation}
  \mathcal{L}\{t\cos(at)\} = \frac{s^2 - a^2}{(s^2 + a^2)^2},
  \eqtitle{Laplace Transform Identity}
  \label{eq:laplace}
\end{equation}
where $s$ is the Laplace-domain variable, $t$ is time, and $a$ is a constant
angular-frequency parameter. The operator $\mathcal{L}$ denotes the Laplace
transform.

Equation~(\ref{eq:laplace}) is used in the following analysis to evaluate the
transformed response.
