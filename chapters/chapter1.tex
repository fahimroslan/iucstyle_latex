\chapter{Introduction}

\section{Purpose}

This sample shows the IUC manuscript format. Replace the sample text with your
research. The institution-wide manual defines the formatting rules for every
faculty and programme.

\section{Research Structure}

Introduce the research problem, objectives, scope, and significance. The
five-chapter sequence is a default: combine or split chapters to suit the
research.

\section{Manuscript Format}

\subsection{Page and Text Settings}

Use A4 pages with margins of 30 mm at the top, 25 mm at the bottom, 38 mm on
the left, and 25 mm on the right. Chapter openings use a 40 mm top margin.

Body text uses Times New Roman, 12 pt, double spacing, a 2 em first-line
indent, and 6 pt after each paragraph. Front matter, tables, captions, and
table or figure notes are single-spaced. References are double-spaced with a
1.27 cm hanging indent and no extra space between entries.

\subsection{Word Counts}

Compulsory minimums and advisory maximums are 10,000 and 30,000 for master's
coursework with a research project; 25,000 and 50,000 for master's mixed mode;
40,000 and 60,000 for master's full research; 40,000 and 60,000 for doctoral
coursework with a dissertation; and 70,000 and 100,000 for PhD full research.
There is no fixed upper word limit.

Count chapter body text, including headings and quotations. Exclude front
matter, abstracts, table and figure content, captions, references, and
appendices. Both abstracts are required; neither has a fixed word limit.
Keywords are optional.

\section{Final Submission}

Submit four hardbound copies and one PDF. Use A4 white paper of at least 80 gsm
printed single-sided. Master's covers are purple; doctoral covers are black.
Use gold lettering. There is no PDF file-size limit. Body text is black; colour
may be used in figures and tables when it improves clarity.
